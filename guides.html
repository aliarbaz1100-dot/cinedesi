Best legal places to watch South Asian films

CineDesi links only to destinations we have verified as official or authorized. Availability can vary by country and over time, so a link means “verified destination,” not a promise that a title is currently playable in every region.

We avoid fake play buttons, unauthorized mirrors, deceptive download pages and redirects designed to imitate streaming services.

What makes a trailer “official”?

We treat a trailer as official when it comes from a rights-holder, studio, distributor, broadcaster or another clearly authorized channel. A random re-upload does not qualify simply because the video looks authentic.

When verification is incomplete, CineDesi keeps the trailer action disabled instead of guessing.

How our rights-safe publishing works

Metadata, posters, trailers and watch destinations are reviewed separately. A movie can stay in draft even when some parts are verified. Public publication is blocked until CineDesi’s database readiness checks are satisfied.

Images need their own source and license review. Official promotional artwork is not automatically treated as reusable just because it appears publicly online.
