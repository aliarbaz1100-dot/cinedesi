TRUST & TRANSPARENCY

Editorial standards

CineDesi publishes concise original editorial context to help people understand what a film is and why it may be worth exploring. These notes are not presented as full critical reviews unless a dedicated review has actually been written.

Sources first

Factual metadata is checked against sources suitable for commercial use or official factual sources. Official trailers and legal watch destinations are verified separately. A movie can be published without a watch link when no suitable destination has been verified for the audience.

No borrowed poster art by default

CineDesi does not treat a publicly visible movie poster as automatically reusable. When commercial reuse rights are not documented, the site uses a rights-safe text placeholder instead.

Editorial independence

Advertising, sponsorships and affiliate relationships do not determine rights checks or editorial wording. Paid placements must be labeled. If CineDesi later introduces scored reviews, commercial relationships will not determine those scores.

Corrections

Availability, release details and platform destinations can change. CineDesi may update a listing when better official evidence becomes available or remove a destination that can no longer be verified.
