Privacy

CineDesi currently uses Supabase for core catalog, admin authentication and newsletter subscriber storage. The public watchlist is stored locally on your device using browser storage.

Newsletter

We store the email address you submit together with your consent status. We use it for CineDesi updates and do not intentionally sell subscriber email addresses.

Partnership inquiries

If you submit the Advertise / Partner form, we store the contact details, company information, inquiry type, message and consent you provide so the authorized CineDesi owner can review the request.

Rights-holder requests

If you submit a rights request, we store the contact details, organization information, request type, referenced movie or page, message, consent and private case-management notes needed to review and respond to the concern.

Analytics and advertising

CineDesi records limited first-party engagement events such as page views, movie views, verified legal-watch clicks, trailer clicks, shares and watchlist additions. These event records are used to understand site performance and do not intentionally include visitor email addresses or IP addresses.

CineDesi also uses Google Analytics 4 to understand traffic and engagement. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and may process device, browser, approximate location and interaction information according to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices. CineDesi does not intentionally send newsletter email addresses or rights-request contact details to Google Analytics.

Google AdSense advertising is not currently enabled. Before advertising that uses cookies or similar technologies is activated, CineDesi will update this policy and the consent experience as required for the regions where the service is offered.

Your choices

You can clear your local watchlist through CineDesi. Newsletter subscribers can submit an unsubscribe request through our public email-preferences page; the request does not publicly reveal whether an address is currently subscribed.
