Advertising & Affiliate Disclosure

CineDesi is an editorial and movie-discovery platform. Some areas of the site may in the future include paid advertising, sponsorships or affiliate links. Any commercial relationship will be clearly identified where it appears.

Advertising

Ad placements, when activated, will be kept separate from editorial judgments. An advertiser does not receive control over CineDesi scores, reviews, source checks or publication decisions.

Sponsorships

Sponsored content or placements will be labeled as sponsored, paid partnership or another clear equivalent. CineDesi will not present a paid placement as an independent editorial recommendation.

Affiliate links

Some verified legal watch links may later use affiliate or referral programs when an official partner program permits it. If a qualifying action can earn CineDesi a commission, the relationship will be disclosed. A commission does not change the price shown by the destination unless that destination says otherwise.

Legal-watch verification

CineDesi only exposes a watch button after a destination has been checked as a legal service for that title. Availability can vary by country and can change over time, so the destination itself remains the final source for current availability and pricing.

Editorial independence

Rights checks, metadata sourcing, trailer verification and poster licensing decisions remain separate from monetization. CineDesi does not accept payment to bypass its publication-readiness rules.
